Columbia 24/7 Plumbing covers the full run of residential and light commercial plumbing around the capital, from a single dripping faucet to a failed sewer lateral. The pages below group the work by what is actually going wrong, so you can jump to the one that matches your problem. Permanent work follows the South Carolina Plumbing Code, which is the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with state modifications.

When water is already where it should not be

A burst pipe and a backed-up main line are the calls we hear in the middle of the night. We clear household-stopping clogs through drain cleaning and fix failed lines through sewer line repair. When the ground is saturated, a sump pump that runs is the difference between a dry crawlspace and a wet floor.

Hot water, tank or tankless

No hot water is usually a part, and water heater repair covers the fixes. A tank that leaks from the seam is a replacement, where sizing and fuel type decide the job. On-demand units fail in their own ways, and tankless service addresses them.

Drains, sewer lines and what protects them

A clog that returns after cabling may need hydro jetting, and a line that has reached the end of its life is a candidate for sewer line replacement. Backflow testing protects the drinking water side, and South Carolina requires a state-certified tester.

Hidden leaks and aging pipe

A bill that jumps with nothing dripping calls for leak detection, and a leak beneath the floor is a slab leak. Water line repair handles the pipe from the meter to the house, and a house with repeat failures may need repiping.

Fixtures, gas and the kitchen

Toilet repair covers running toilets, leaking bases and rocking bowls. Drips and under-sink leaks belong to faucet and sink repair, and a jammed garbage disposal is usually a quick fix. Bathroom projects fall under shower and bathtub installation, and gas line installation and repair covers new appliance hookups.

Water quality, drainage and commercial properties

Columbia city water is soft, so water softener advice starts with a test. Chloramine taste is better addressed with water filtration. Basement waterproofing covers wet crawlspaces and basements and explains what a plumber handles. Commercial plumbing serves restaurants, offices and rentals.

After the water has done its damage

Drying and rebuilding a wet home is a separate service from fixing the pipe. See water damage restoration and water mitigation. Those two pages have their own phone line, shown only on those pages.

What to do while you wait for a plumber

  • 1Close the main water valve. It is usually near the meter or where the supply line enters the house.
  • 2Stay out of wet rooms with electrical hazards. Leave the breaker panel alone if you would have to stand in water to reach it.
  • 3Leave if you smell gas. Go outside, then call 911 and your gas provider before you call anyone else.
