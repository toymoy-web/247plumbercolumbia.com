The emergencies Irmo houses are set up for

Irmo has a lot of houses from the 1980s through the 2000s, and the first generation of their water heaters is now at replacement age. A tank that leaks from the seam, not from a fitting, is finished, and a water heater replacement the same day is cheaper than the floor it floods.

Houses from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s add one more thing to check. Some builders in that window used gray polybutylene supply pipe, which is known for failing at its fittings. If you see gray plastic pipe at the water heater or main shutoff, have it looked at, and ask about repiping before it leaks inside a wall. Before we arrive: a burst pipe or active leak at your Irmo house or lake house

A leak in Irmo can mean a family home with a running meter or a lake house nobody has opened in weeks. The first four steps are the same.

1

Find and close the main

In many Irmo subdivisions the main valve sits in the garage or utility room, while some lake houses have it in a meter box near the road. Close it, then check the meter with every fixture off to see whether water still moves. Your bill names your water provider, and a break on the street side of the meter goes to that provider.

2

Turn off the water heater

If the heater is the source or the tank is draining, switch an electric unit off at its breaker so the elements do not burn out dry. Turn a gas control to off, and do not relight a pilot in a wet closet. Leave breakers on any wet circuit off until the area dries.

3

Clear and document

Move belongings out of the path of the water and photograph everything, especially in a second home where damage may sit unseen for days. Note the time you found it. Stay off wet floors near outlets.

4

Call the 24/7 line

What changes the price of a water heater or repipe job

Water heater work in Irmo varies on a few things you can check yourself. Fuel type comes first, since gas units need a vent and a gas connection that must pass inspection, while electric units need the right circuit. Tank size matters, and so does the choice to move to a tankless unit, which often needs a larger gas line and new venting.

Location changes the labor as well. A heater in a garage is a quick swap, and one in an attic or a tight closet takes longer. For a repipe, the number of fixtures and whether the walls are open or finished drive the total more than the footage of pipe does. You hear the number before work starts.

Lake Murray houses that sit empty

Lake Murray's dam was finished in 1930 and was the largest earthen dam in the world at the time, built of local red clay and bedrock. The lake made Irmo a gateway for second homes, and many of them sit empty for weeks, which is exactly when a small leak turns into a large one.

A slow leak in an empty house can run for a month before anyone notices. Shut off the main when you leave, and when you return, check the meter with everything off. If it moves, book leak detection before you unpack. When you call, tell the technician how long the house has been empty and where the main valve is, and ask for the price of a full-house leak check before the visit.
